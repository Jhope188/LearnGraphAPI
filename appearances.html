MS Learn · Featured

SMS & Voice Retirement: Passkeys by Default

Microsoft is retiring native SMS and voice MFA in Entra ID. Starting September 1, 2026, users enabled for SMS or voice are automatically enrolled for passkey registration. Full retirement of Microsoft-provided telecom delivery lands February 1, 2027.

This MS Learn walkthrough covers the retirement timeline, migration paths including the passkey registration campaign and customer-managed telecom providers, and how to use Graph API to opt out of automatic enablement during the transition window.
