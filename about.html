My story

I am an M365 Solutions Architect with 15 years of experience across the MSP landscape and datacenter technologies, including six years leading Azure migrations. Now in my role at Inforcer, I focus on security configuration management across the M365 ecosystem, spanning Entra Identity, Intune, Purview, SharePoint, and Microsoft Defender.

Going from managing on-premises infrastructure to guiding organizations through cloud transformation gives me a rare ability to bridge Azure and identity security. I understand not just how systems are configured, but why they behave the way they do, and what happens when they don't.

Identity is the foundation, and Conditional Access is the perimeter. I approach every engagement with that lens, ensuring policy intent translates into real, enforceable protection.

Beyond the technical work, I'm driven by a genuine desire to give back. The same curiosity and community that shaped my career now fuel my commitment to helping others navigate modern cloud security.
