## CHARMS

### Accio (Summoning Charm)
- **Incantation:** Accio [object name]
- **🗣️ Pronunciation:** AH-key-oh (stress on the FIRST syllable — NOT "ah-SEE-oh")
- **Type:** Charm
- **Difficulty:** O.W.L. Level (4th Year)
- **Effect:** Summons an object to the caster from a distance. The caster must clearly visualise the object.
- **Wand Movement:** Sharp flick toward self
- **🪄 Wand Motion Step-by-Step:**
  1. Extend wand arm forward, pointing toward the object (or its general direction) →
  2. Sharp flick backward toward yourself ← (like beckoning the object to come)
  3. Hold wand steady at chest height — the object will fly to your hand
  - *Timing:* Say "AH" on the forward point, "key-oh" on the flick back
- **Use When:** You need to retrieve something far away, lost items, or objects out of reach.
- **Famous Use:** Harry Potter summoned his Firebolt during the First Task of the Triwizard Tournament to outfly the Hungarian Horntail.
- **Counter:** Cannot summon objects protected by Anti-Theft Charms (e.g. Gringotts vaults).
- **Teaching Notes:** Taught by Professor Flitwick in 4th year Charms class. Requires strong concentration on the desired object.

### Aguamenti (Water-Making Spell)
- **Incantation:** Aguamenti
- **🗣️ Pronunciation:** AH-gwah-MEN-tee (four syllables, stress on the third)
- **Type:** Charm / Conjuration
- **Difficulty:** N.E.W.T. Level (6th Year)
- **Effect:** Produces a jet of clean, drinkable water from the wand tip.
- **Wand Movement:** Smooth wave motion
- **🪄 Wand Motion Step-by-Step:**
  1. Hold wand at chest height, tip pointing slightly downward ↘
  2. Sweep wand in a gentle S-shaped wave motion, like the flow of water ∿→
  3. Push forward on the final syllable "tee" → — water streams from the tip
  - *Timing:* Begin the wave on "AH-gwah", push forward on "MEN-tee"
- **Use When:** You need water for drinking, extinguishing fires, filling containers, or cleaning.
- **Famous Use:** Dumbledore attempted to use it to give water to a weakened individual in the Crystal Cave, but the enchantment made the water vanish.
- **Counter:** Certain dark enchantments can cause conjured water to vanish instantly.
- **Teaching Notes:** Part of the 6th year curriculum. One of the few conjuration spells that creates a consumable substance.

### Alohomora (Unlocking Charm)
- **Incantation:** Alohomora
- **🗣️ Pronunciation:** AL-oh-ho-MOR-ah (five syllables, stress on the fourth — let the "MOR" ring out)
- **Type:** Charm
- **Difficulty:** Beginner (1st Year)
- **Effect:** Unlocks doors and windows that have been sealed with a standard locking spell.
- **Wand Movement:** Backward S shape
- **🪄 Wand Motion Step-by-Step:**
  1. Point wand at the lock from arm's length →
  2. Trace a backward S shape in the air (start top-right ↗, curve left ↙, curve right ↘)
  3. Finish with a gentle tap on the lock — you should hear the click
  - *Timing:* Trace the S during "AL-oh-ho", tap on "MOR-ah"
- **Use When:** You need to open a locked door, chest, window, or container that uses a standard magical lock.
- **Famous Use:** Hermione used it to unlock the door to the forbidden third-floor corridor where Fluffy guarded the Philosopher's Stone.
- **Counter:** Does not work against more advanced magical locks or the Colloportus charm when reinforced. Anti-Alohomora charms exist.
- **Teaching Notes:** One of the first spells taught at Hogwarts. Originated in Africa before being brought to Britain by Eldon Elsrickle.

### Aparecium (Revealing Charm)
- **Incantation:** Aparecium
- **🗣️ Pronunciation:** AH-pah-REE-see-um (five syllables, stress on the third)
- **Type:** Charm
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate (3rd Year)
- **Effect:** Reveals invisible ink or concealed writing on parchment or other surfaces.
- **Wand Movement:** Tap on the surface
- **🪄 Wand Motion Step-by-Step:**
  1. Hold wand vertically above the parchment/surface ↓
  2. Tap the surface firmly three times — tap, tap, tap ↓↓↓
  3. On the third tap, hold the wand tip against the surface and speak the incantation
  - *Timing:* Three quick taps, then the incantation on the hold
- **Use When:** You suspect hidden messages, invisible ink, or concealed text on documents.
- **Famous Use:** Hermione attempted to use it on Tom Riddle's diary, though the diary's dark magic prevented it from working.
- **Counter:** Extremely powerful concealment magic may resist this charm.

### Arresto Momentum (Slowing Charm)
- **Incantation:** Arresto Momentum
- **🗣️ Pronunciation:** ah-REST-oh mo-MEN-tum (stress on REST and MEN — say it with authority)
- **Type:** Charm
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate (3rd Year)
- **Effect:** Slows the speed of a moving object or person. Can prevent injury from falls.
- **Wand Movement:** Sweeping downward motion
- **🪄 Wand Motion Step-by-Step:**
  1. Raise wand high above head ↑
  2. Sweep downward in a firm, controlled arc toward the falling object ↓
  3. Push palm of free hand forward (stop gesture) while holding wand steady
  - *Timing:* "ah-REST-oh" on the downward sweep, "mo-MEN-tum" on the hold
- **Use When:** Someone or something is falling, an object is moving too fast, you need to slow a collision.
- **Famous Use:** Dumbledore used it to slow Harry's fall during a Quidditch match when Dementors attacked.
- **Counter:** Very heavy or fast objects may only be partially slowed.

### Cheering Charm
- **Incantation:** (Non-verbal, intention-based)
- **Type:** Charm
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate (3rd Year)
- **Effect:** Causes the target to become cheerful and happy. Excessive use can cause uncontrollable laughter.
- **Wand Movement:** Gentle upward flick
- **Use When:** Someone is feeling down, needs encouragement, or you want to lighten the mood.
- **Famous Use:** Harry excelled at this charm in his 3rd year exams under Professor Flitwick.
- **Teaching Notes:** Must be applied carefully — too much force causes hysterical giggling that takes hours to wear off.

### Confundo (Confundus Charm)
- **Incantation:** Confundo
- **🗣️ Pronunciation:** con-FUN-doh (three syllables, stress on the second — rhymes with "fun")
- **Type:** Charm
- **Difficulty:** O.W.L. Level (5th Year)
- **Effect:** Causes the target to become confused, disoriented, and suggestible.
- **Wand Movement:** Spiral motion toward the target
- **🪄 Wand Motion Step-by-Step:**
  1. Point wand at the target →
  2. Trace a tight clockwise spiral ↻ (3 rotations) while pushing forward
  3. Jab forward sharply on the final syllable → to release the charm
  - *Timing:* Begin spiral on "con", accelerate through "FUN", jab on "doh"
- **Use When:** You need to misdirect someone, cause temporary confusion, or make someone susceptible to suggestion.
- **Famous Use:** Hermione used it on Cormac McLaggen during Quidditch Keeper tryouts to help Ron.
- **Counter:** Can be shaken off by those with strong willpower. Effects wear off naturally.
- **Legal Note:** Using a Confundus Charm on a Muggle is a violation of the Statute of Secrecy.

### Engorgio (Engorgement Charm)
- **Incantation:** Engorgio
- **🗣️ Pronunciation:** en-GOR-jee-oh (four syllables, stress on the second — make the "GOR" big and bold)
- **Type:** Charm
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate (4th Year)
- **Effect:** Causes the target object or creature to swell in size.
- **Wand Movement:** Upward expanding gesture
- **🪄 Wand Motion Step-by-Step:**
  1. Point wand at the target object at arm's length →
  2. Lift wand upward while spreading outward ↑↗ (as if the air is expanding)
  3. Hold at the peak — the object swells to the desired size
  - *Timing:* Begin lifting on "en-GOR", spread outward on "jee-oh"
- **Use When:** You need to enlarge an object, make something bigger for visibility, or increase the size of ingredients.
- **Counter Spell:** Reducio (Shrinking Charm)
- **Famous Use:** Hagrid was suspected of using it on his pumpkins for the Halloween feast.

### Episkey (Healing Spell)
- **Incantation:** Episkey
- **🗣️ Pronunciation:** eh-PIS-key (three syllables, stress on the second — crisp and precise)
- **Type:** Healing Charm
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate (3rd Year)
- **Effect:** Heals minor injuries such as broken noses, split lips, and small cuts.
- **Wand Movement:** Touch wand tip to injury site
- **🪄 Wand Motion Step-by-Step:**
  1. Hold wand gently, tip downward toward the injury ↓
  2. Lightly touch the wand tip to the injured area — DO NOT press hard
  3. Small clockwise circle ↻ over the wound while speaking the incantation
  - *Timing:* Touch on "eh", circle on "PIS-key" — warmth should radiate from the tip
- **Use When:** Someone has a minor injury — broken nose, small cuts, bruises, sprains.
- **Famous Use:** Tonks used it to heal Harry's broken nose on the Hogwarts Express in his 6th year.
- **Limitations:** Cannot heal serious injuries, cursed wounds, or re-grow bones.

### Expecto Patronum (Patronus Charm)
- **Incantation:** Expecto Patronum
- **🗣️ Pronunciation:** ex-PEK-toh pah-TROH-num (six syllables across two words — stress on PEK and TROH, let it ring with conviction)
- **Type:** Charm (Advanced Defensive)
- **Difficulty:** N.E.W.T. Level / Beyond (Extremely Advanced)
- **Effect:** Conjures a Patronus — a silver guardian made of pure positive energy. Corporeal Patronuses take animal form. Protects against Dementors and Lethifolds.
- **Wand Movement:** Circular motion while focusing on happiest memory
- **🪄 Wand Motion Step-by-Step:**
  1. Close eyes briefly — summon your HAPPIEST, most powerful memory 💭
  2. Raise wand to chest height, arm extended forward →
  3. Trace a large clockwise circle ↻ (representing the shield of positivity)
  4. On completing the circle, thrust wand forward → with full conviction
  5. Silver light erupts from the tip — hold your focus on the memory
  - *Timing:* Circle during "ex-PEK-toh", thrust forward on "pah-TROH-num"
  - *Critical:* The strength of the Patronus depends ENTIRELY on the strength of your memory. Wavering focus = wispy, ineffective mist
- **Use When:** Dementors are near, you need protection from dark creatures, or you need to send a message (Patronus messaging).
- **Famous Use:** Harry produced a full corporeal stag Patronus at age 13, driving away over a hundred Dementors at the Black Lake.
- **Critical Requirement:** Requires focusing on your happiest, most powerful positive memory. The strength of the Patronus depends on the strength of the memory.
- **Patronus Forms (Known):** Stag (Harry Potter), Otter (Hermione Granger), Jack Russell Terrier (Ron Weasley), Doe (Severus Snape/Lily Potter), Phoenix (Albus Dumbledore), Horse (Ginny Weasley), Hare (Luna Lovegood), Cat (Minerva McGonagall).
- **Teaching Notes:** Many adult wizards cannot produce a corporeal Patronus. Remus Lupin taught Harry this spell in his 3rd year. It is considered one of the most advanced defensive spells in existence.

### Expelliarmus (Disarming Charm)
- **Incantation:** Expelliarmus
- **🗣️ Pronunciation:** ex-PELL-ee-AR-mus (five syllables — stress on PELL and AR, say it fast and sharp like a whip crack)
- **Type:** Charm (Defensive/Duelling)
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate (2nd Year)
- **Effect:** Forces the target to release whatever they are holding, typically disarming them of their wand.
- **Wand Movement:** Sharp forward jab
- **🪄 Wand Motion Step-by-Step:**
  1. Pull wand back to shoulder, elbow bent (loading position) ←
  2. Sharp, fast jab forward toward the target's wand hand → (like a fencing lunge)
  3. Flick wrist upward ↑ at the end of the jab — this is what rips the wand free
  - *Timing:* Pull back on "ex", jab on "PELL-ee", flick up on "AR-mus"
  - *Speed matters:* This spell rewards FAST casting — hesitation lets your opponent shield
- **Use When:** Duelling, self-defence, disarming an opponent, preventing someone from casting a spell.
- **Famous Use:** Harry's signature spell. He used it to defeat Voldemort in their final duel, causing the Elder Wand to rebound Voldemort's Killing Curse.
- **Teaching Notes:** Taught by Gilderoy Lockhart (demonstrated by Snape) in the Duelling Club. Despite being considered "basic," it proved to be one of the most effective combat spells when mastered.

### Feather-Light Charm
- **Incantation:** (Non-verbal)
- **🗣️ Pronunciation:** Non-verbal — cast through focused intent (think "light as a feather" while pointing at the object)
- **Type:** Charm
- **Difficulty:** Beginner (1st Year)
- **Effect:** Makes objects lighter and easier to carry without changing their size.
- **Wand Movement:** Gentle upward lift motion
- **🪄 Wand Motion Step-by-Step:**
  1. Point wand at the heavy object ↓
  2. Gentle upward lift ↑ (as if the object is already weightless)
  3. The object retains its size but becomes feather-light 🪶
  - *Timing:* Non-verbal — focus your mind on lightness while lifting
- **Use When:** You need to carry heavy luggage, move furniture, transport supplies.
- **Famous Use:** Students commonly use it on their school trunks before the journey to Hogwarts.

### Lumos (Wand-Lighting Charm)
- **Incantation:** Lumos
- **🗣️ Pronunciation:** LOO-mos (two syllables, stress on the first — short and confident, like switching on a light)
- **Type:** Charm
- **Difficulty:** Beginner (1st Year)
- **Effect:** Creates a bright light at the tip of the caster's wand. Lumos Maxima creates a blinding flash.
- **Wand Movement:** Simple upward flick
- **🪄 Wand Motion Step-by-Step:**
  1. Hold wand at waist height, tip pointing down ↓
  2. Quick upward flick ↑ — like flicking a light switch
  3. Hold wand aloft — the tip glows steadily ✨
  - *Timing:* Flick on "LOO", light appears on "mos"
  - *For Lumos Maxima:* Same motion but STRONGER flick, ending with wand thrust upward ↑↑
- **Use When:** You need light in dark places, exploring, reading in the dark, navigating at night.
- **Counter Spell:** Nox (extinguishes the light)
- **Variations:** Lumos Maxima (intense flash), Lumos Solem (sunlight beam — effective against Devil's Snare)
- **Famous Use:** Hermione used Lumos Solem to escape Devil's Snare in the chamber protecting the Philosopher's Stone.

### Obliviate (Memory Charm)
- **Incantation:** Obliviate
- **🗣️ Pronunciation:** oh-BLIV-ee-ate (four syllables, stress on the second — let the "BLIV" carry weight and finality)
- **Type:** Charm
- **Difficulty:** Advanced (N.E.W.T. Level)
- **Effect:** Erases specific memories from the target's mind. Can be precisely targeted to remove only certain memories.
- **Wand Movement:** Pointed at the temple
- **🪄 Wand Motion Step-by-Step:**
  1. Point wand directly at the target's temple (right side) →
  2. Slow, deliberate counter-clockwise circle ↺ (unwinding the memory)
  3. Pull wand gently away ← as if drawing the memory out like a silver thread
  - *Timing:* Point on "oh", circle on "BLIV-ee", pull away on "ate"
  - *Precision required:* Think ONLY of the specific memory to erase — stray thoughts cause collateral damage
- **Use When:** Someone has witnessed magical events who shouldn't have (Statute of Secrecy), or to protect sensitive information.
- **Famous Use:** Hermione used it on her parents to protect them from Death Eaters, making them forget they had a daughter.
- **Warning:** Improperly cast Memory Charms can cause permanent, irreversible brain damage. Gilderoy Lockhart's own Memory Charm backfired, leaving him permanently amnesiac.
- **Legal Note:** Ministry-approved Obliviators are specifically trained to perform this charm safely.

### Protego (Shield Charm)
- **Incantation:** Protego
- **🗣️ Pronunciation:** pro-TAY-goh (three syllables, stress on the second — firm and commanding, like raising a wall)
- **Type:** Charm (Defensive)
- **Difficulty:** O.W.L. Level (5th Year)
- **Effect:** Creates an invisible magical shield that deflects spells and physical entities.
- **Wand Movement:** Broad sweeping motion
- **🪄 Wand Motion Step-by-Step:**
  1. Hold wand vertically in front of you, tip up ↑
  2. Sweep wand from left to right → in a broad horizontal arc (drawing the shield)
  3. End with wand braced at centre-chest, tip pointing forward →
  - *Timing:* Sweep on "pro-TAY", brace on "goh"
  - *For Protego Maxima:* Larger, more forceful sweep — use your whole arm, not just the wrist
- **Use When:** Under attack, duelling, protecting yourself or others from incoming spells.
- **Variations:** Protego Totalum (area shield), Protego Horribilis (powerful shield against dark magic), Protego Maxima (maximum strength shield)
- **Famous Use:** The combined Protego spells were used to create the magical shield around Hogwarts during the Battle of Hogwarts.
- **Limitations:** Cannot block Unforgivable Curses. Very powerful spells may shatter the shield.

### Reducto (Reductor Curse)
- **Incantation:** Reducto
- **🗣️ Pronunciation:** reh-DUK-toh (three syllables, stress on the second — say "DUK" with a hard, explosive punch)
- **Type:** Curse
- **Difficulty:** O.W.L. Level (4th Year)
- **Effect:** Blasts solid objects into pieces. More powerful versions can reduce objects to dust.
- **Wand Movement:** Forceful forward thrust
- **🪄 Wand Motion Step-by-Step:**
  1. Pull wand back behind shoulder ← (coiling energy)
  2. Powerful forward thrust → toward the target (like throwing a javelin)
  3. Sharp stop — the impact blasts the object on contact 💥
  - *Timing:* Pull back on "reh", thrust on "DUK-toh"
  - *Power tip:* The HARDER the thrust, the more destructive the blast
- **Use When:** You need to break through barriers, destroy obstacles, blast open doors.
- **Famous Use:** Ginny Weasley's Reductor Curse was so powerful it impressed the D.A. members during practice.

### Reparo (Mending Charm)
- **Incantation:** Reparo
- **🗣️ Pronunciation:** reh-PAH-roh (three syllables, stress on the second — gentle and restorative, like mending itself)
- **Type:** Charm
- **Difficulty:** Beginner (1st Year)
- **Effect:** Repairs broken objects, restoring them to their original state.
- **Wand Movement:** Tap on the broken object
- **🪄 Wand Motion Step-by-Step:**
  1. Point wand at the broken object ↓
  2. Gentle tap on the largest fragment — one clean tap
  3. Trace a slow clockwise circle ↻ over the pieces (gathering them together)
  - *Timing:* Tap on "reh", circle on "PAH-roh" — pieces fly back together
- **Use When:** Something is broken, shattered, torn, or damaged and needs to be fixed.
- **Famous Use:** Hermione used it frequently, including to fix Harry's glasses on the Hogwarts Express.
- **Limitations:** Cannot repair objects destroyed by very powerful dark magic. Cannot repair wands (except with the Elder Wand).

### Riddikulus (Boggart-Banishing Spell)
- **Incantation:** Riddikulus
- **🗣️ Pronunciation:** rih-DIK-yoo-lus (four syllables, stress on the second — say it with AMUSEMENT, almost laughing)
- **Type:** Charm (Defence)
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate (3rd Year)
- **Effect:** Forces a Boggart to assume a humorous form, which weakens it through laughter.
- **Wand Movement:** Short, sharp flick
- **🪄 Wand Motion Step-by-Step:**
  1. Face the Boggart — visualise something FUNNY 😂
  2. Raise wand to eye level →
  3. Short, sharp flick ↗ (like cracking a tiny whip)
  - *Timing:* Flick on "DIK" — the humour transforms the Boggart instantly
  - *Critical:* Your MENTAL IMAGE matters more than the wand motion. If you can't imagine it funny, the spell fizzles
- **Use When:** Confronting a Boggart (a shapeshifter that takes the form of your worst fear).
- **Critical Requirement:** You must clearly visualise something funny while casting. Laughter is what truly defeats the Boggart.
- **Famous Use:** Neville Longbottom imagined Professor Snape in his grandmother's clothes.
- **Teaching Notes:** Taught by Professor Lupin in 3rd year Defence Against the Dark Arts.

### Silencio (Silencing Charm)
- **Incantation:** Silencio
- **🗣️ Pronunciation:** sih-LEN-see-oh (four syllables, stress on the second — say it softly, almost whispering, ironic given its effect)
- **Type:** Charm
- **Difficulty:** O.W.L. Level (5th Year)
- **Effect:** Renders the target unable to make sound. Works on creatures and objects.
- **Wand Movement:** Sharp downward slash
- **🪄 Wand Motion Step-by-Step:**
  1. Raise wand above head ↑
  2. Sharp downward slash ↓ (like chopping the sound in half)
  3. Point wand at the target and hold — silence falls
  - *Timing:* Slash on "LEN", point on "see-oh"
- **Use When:** You need silence, to prevent someone from casting verbal spells, to quiet a noisy creature.
- **Famous Use:** Hermione successfully cast it on a raven during O.W.L. practice.

### Sonorus (Amplifying Charm)
- **Incantation:** Sonorus
- **🗣️ Pronunciation:** soh-NOR-us (three syllables, stress on the second — let your voice grow louder as you say it)
- **Type:** Charm
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Effect:** Magically amplifies the caster's voice so it can be heard over great distances.
- **Wand Movement:** Touch wand to throat
- **🪄 Wand Motion Step-by-Step:**
  1. Raise wand to your own throat ↑
  2. Lightly touch wand tip to the base of your neck
  3. Hold contact — your voice amplifies immediately 🔊
  - *Timing:* Touch on "soh-NOR", amplification activates on "us"
  - *To cancel:* Say "Quietus" with the same gesture
- **Use When:** Public speaking, Quidditch commentary, addressing large crowds, emergencies.
- **Counter Spell:** Quietus (returns voice to normal volume)
- **Famous Use:** Ludo Bagman used it to commentate at the Quidditch World Cup.

### Wingardium Leviosa (Levitation Charm)
- **Incantation:** Wingardium Leviosa
- **🗣️ Pronunciation:** win-GAR-dee-um lev-ee-OH-sa (seven syllables across two words — stress on GAR and OH. Remember: "It's Levi-OH-sa, not Levio-SAH!")
- **Type:** Charm
- **Difficulty:** Beginner (1st Year)
- **Effect:** Makes objects levitate and float under the caster's control.
- **Wand Movement:** Swish and flick
- **🪄 Wand Motion Step-by-Step:**
  1. Hold wand horizontally, tip pointing at the object →
  2. SWISH — smooth horizontal sweep to the right → (drawing a line in the air)
  3. FLICK — crisp upward flick ↑ at the end of the swish
  4. The object rises — control height with gentle up/down wand tilts
  - *Timing:* Swish on "win-GAR-dee-um", flick UP on "lev-ee-OH-sa"
  - *Common mistake:* Saying "Levio-SAH" instead of "Levi-OH-sa" — results in nothing (or a fire, if you're Seamus)
- **Use When:** You need to lift or move objects without touching them, reaching high places, moving heavy items.
- **Famous Use:** Ron used it to levitate a troll's club and knock out the mountain troll in the girls' bathroom.
- **Teaching Notes:** The very first spell taught in Charms class by Professor Flitwick. "It's Levi-OH-sa, not Levio-SAH!" — Hermione Granger.
- **Critical Note:** Pronunciation is crucial. Seamus Finnigan set his feather on fire by getting it wrong.

## TRANSFIGURATION SPELLS

### Avifors
- **Incantation:** Avifors
- **🗣️ Pronunciation:** AH-vih-fors (three syllables, stress on the first — light and airy, like bird wings)
- **Type:** Transfiguration
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate (3rd Year)
- **Effect:** Transforms small objects into birds.
- **Wand Movement:** Tap the object, then flick upward
- **🪄 Wand Motion Step-by-Step:**
  1. Point wand at the target object →
  2. Firm tap on the object (transferring transfiguration energy)
  3. Quick upward flick ↑ — the object takes flight as a bird 🐦
  - *Timing:* Tap on "AH-vih", flick on "fors"

### Draconifors
- **Incantation:** Draconifors
- **🗣️ Pronunciation:** dra-KOH-nih-fors (four syllables, stress on the second — commanding and fierce, like taming a dragon)
- **Type:** Transfiguration
- **Difficulty:** Advanced (5th Year)
- **Effect:** Transforms small objects into miniature dragons that attack the nearest target.
- **Wand Movement:** Tap the object, then aggressive forward thrust
- **🪄 Wand Motion Step-by-Step:**
  1. Point wand at the target object →
  2. Sharp tap on the object (igniting the transformation)
  3. Aggressive forward thrust → toward the enemy — the dragon launches 🐉
  - *Timing:* Tap on "dra-KOH", thrust on "nih-fors"
  - *Caution:* The miniature dragon is NOT under your control — it attacks the nearest living target

### Lapifors
- **Incantation:** Lapifors
- **🗣️ Pronunciation:** LAP-ih-fors (three syllables, stress on the first — quick and bouncy, like a hopping rabbit)
- **Type:** Transfiguration
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate (3rd Year)
- **Effect:** Transforms small objects into rabbits.
- **Wand Movement:** Tap the object, then gentle flick forward
- **🪄 Wand Motion Step-by-Step:**
  1. Point wand at the target object →
  2. Gentle tap on the object
  3. Soft forward flick ↗ (a little hop motion, like the rabbit will make) 🐇
  - *Timing:* Tap on "LAP", flick on "ih-fors"

### Vera Verto
- **Incantation:** Vera Verto
- **🗣️ Pronunciation:** VEH-rah VER-toh (four syllables across two words, stress on the first syllable of each — crisp and academic)
- **Type:** Transfiguration
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate (2nd Year)
- **Effect:** Transforms an animal into a water goblet.
- **Wand Movement:** Three taps on the animal
- **🪄 Wand Motion Step-by-Step:**
  1. Hold wand over the animal ↓
  2. Three deliberate taps: tap — tap — tap (evenly spaced, firm but not hard)
  3. On the third tap, the animal transforms into a goblet 🏆
  - *Timing:* First tap on "VEH", second tap on "rah", third tap on "VER-toh"
  - *Common mistake:* Ron's broken wand caused the goblet to retain fur — all three taps must carry equal energy
- **Use When:** Transfiguration class demonstrations, practical examinations.
- **Famous Use:** Professor McGonagall demonstrated this in class. Ron attempted it but his wand malfunctioned, producing a furry goblet.

## DEFENSIVE SPELLS

### Cave Inimicum
- **Incantation:** Cave Inimicum
- **🗣️ Pronunciation:** KAH-vay ih-NIM-ih-kum (five syllables across two words — stress on KAH and NIM, spoken with quiet authority like setting a boundary)
- **Type:** Protective Charm
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Effect:** Creates a protective boundary that warns of and deters enemies from an area.
- **Wand Movement:** Sweep wand in a wide arc tracing the perimeter
- **🪄 Wand Motion Step-by-Step:**
  1. Extend wand outward → toward the edge of the area you want protected
  2. Walk slowly, sweeping the wand in a wide arc ↻ tracing the boundary
  3. Complete the circle — the ward settles invisibly into place

## SPELL CREATION GUIDELINES

When requesting a NEW spell, the Spellcaster will create one following these magical principles:

### Latin/Greek Root Construction
Most spells derive from Latin, Greek, or a combination. The incantation should:
- Sound magical and authoritative
- Relate directly to the spell's effect
- Follow established phonetic patterns of known spells
- Include a **🗣️ Pronunciation guide** with syllable breakdown and stress pattern (stressed syllable in CAPS)

### Wand Movement Classification
All new spells MUST include a **🪄 Wand Motion Step-by-Step** section with:
- Numbered directional steps using arrows (→ ← ↑ ↓ ↗ ↘ ↻ ↺)
- Timing notes linking wand motion to syllables of the incantation
- Tips or common mistakes where applicable

Standard wand movement categories:
- **Jab/Thrust:** Offensive spells
- **Flick:** Quick effect spells, charms
- **Swish:** Flowing, continuous effects
- **Circular:** Area effects, protection
